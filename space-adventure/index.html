A short film by Chris Beach

How it was made

Every shot in the film was generated by AI on a single home PC with an NVIDIA RTX 5090. Nothing was rendered in the cloud.

The model

The video model is MiniMax H3, run locally in its reference-to-video mode (REF2VA). Rather than starting from a text prompt alone, each shot is built from a set of reference pictures: photos of the family, plus images of the spaceship, the cockpit, the cabin, the spacesuit and Freddie's toy giraffe. The prompt then says which picture is which subject, for example "Subject 1 is the 7 year old boy in Pictures 1, 2 and 3", and describes the action shot by shot. The model keeps those people and objects recognisable across the whole clip.

The workflow

  • ComfyUI runs the generation, using a workflow adapted from the community DaSiWa MiniMax H3 workflow, with one saved workflow per scene.
  • Each clip is generated at 1376×768 and 24 fps (25 sampling steps, res_multistep sampler), with a Qwen3-VL 32B text encoder reading the prompt.
  • It is then upscaled 1.5× to 2080×1152 with NVIDIA RTX Video Super Resolution, and frame-interpolated to 48 fps with FILM.
  • A 12-second clip takes about 10 minutes to render on the RTX 5090.
  • Each shot took several attempts. I rendered variations, reviewed them, and kept the best take of each scene.

The edit

The clips were cut together in DaVinci Resolve Studio on a 48 fps timeline and set to music: Snowfall by Øneheart and reidenshi.

Tools

  • MiniMax H3 (video generation)
  • ComfyUI (generation workflow)
  • DaVinci Resolve Studio 21 (editing)
  • FFmpeg (encoding for the web)
  • Ubuntu Linux with an NVIDIA RTX 5090
